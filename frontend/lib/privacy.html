Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Medicine Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name and account information.
  • Patient and caretaker information.
  • Medicine names and medication schedules.
  • Medication usage and history.
  • Photographs of medicines uploaded by users.
  • Reminder and notification preferences.
  • Information you voluntarily provide to the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide medication tracking features.
  • Create and manage patient profiles.
  • Display medication history.
  • Provide medication reminders.
  • Process uploaded medicine images.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the App.
  • Protect the security of the App and its users.

4. Medicine Images

If you upload an image of a medicine, the image may be processed to identify or extract information about the medicine.

Privacy note: Do not upload photographs containing unnecessary personal, financial, identification, or other sensitive information.

5. AI-Generated Information

Some features may use artificial intelligence to analyze medicine images or provide informational descriptions of medicines.

AI-generated information may be inaccurate. It should not be treated as medical advice and should be verified using reliable medical sources or with a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help us operate the App, such as hosting, database, authentication, notification, or AI-processing providers, where necessary to provide App functionality.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of certain personal information.

You may contact us through the contact information provided within the App to request assistance with your information.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization from a parent or legal guardian.

11. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, analytics, notifications, databases, or AI processing. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through the contact information provided within the App.
